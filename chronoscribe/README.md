# Chronoscribe AI: The Autonomous Content Lifecycle Engine

Chronoscribe AI is an intelligent system designed to autonomously manage the entire lifecycle of an organization's content assets, from inception through continuous optimization to intelligent archiving or retirement.

## Primary Function

Chronoscribe AI acts as the central nervous system for content, ensuring every piece remains relevant, effective, and compliant throughout its operational existence. Its primary function is to autonomously monitor, evaluate, update, and manage the disposition of content based on real-time performance metrics, predefined business rules, and evolving contextual understanding.

## Key AI Technologies

- **Generative AI (LLMs & Diffusion Models)**: Creation, Optimization, and Personalization of content.
- **NLU & NLP**: Analysis, Compliance, Metadata Generation, and Competitor Analysis.
- **Machine Learning (Predictive Analytics & RL)**: Performance Forecasting, Anomaly Detection, Recommendation Engine, and Adaptive Optimization.
- **Knowledge Graphs & Semantic Web**: Contextual Intelligence and Dependency Mapping.
- **Computer Vision**: Image/Video Analysis and Brand Consistency.

## Infrastructure

This project is built on top of the **Genesis Q-Mem Stack**, utilizing:
- **LLM Server**: Phi-2 model running via `llama.cpp` for text generation.
- **Redis (Q-Mem Cache)**: For high-speed data access and potentially Knowledge Graph storage.
- **Monitoring**: Real-time health and performance tracking.
